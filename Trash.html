Sorting & Disposal Guidelines
General Waste
Non-recyclable household waste, sealed in bags. No liquids, batteries, or electronics.
Recyclables
Rinse and dry plastics, glass, cans, and paper before placing in the bin. Flatten cartons.
Organic Waste
Food scraps and garden trimmings. Keep separate from plastic packaging.
Hazardous Materials
Batteries, paint, chemicals — request a special pickup, never place in regular bins.
