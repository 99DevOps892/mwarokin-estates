Platform Features

Everything you need to run a modern portfolio

From a single rental to a 500-unit portfolio, Mwarokin Estates scales with you — handling the complexity so you can focus on growth.

Smart Dashboard
Real-time portfolio overview with income trends, occupancy heatmaps, and AI-powered forecasts for every property and unit.
Tenant Screening
Instant background checks, credit reports, employment verification, and landlord references — all in one automated flow.
Rent Collection
M-Pesa, Airtel Money, bank transfers, and card payments. Auto-reminders, receipts, and real-time deposit tracking built in.
Digital Leases
E-sign compliant lease agreements, automatic renewals, custom clauses, and a complete document vault per tenant.
Maintenance Hub
Tenants submit issues via app or WhatsApp. Auto-assign to vendors, track progress, and capture photo evidence at every step.
Financial Reports
P&L statements, cash flow analysis, expense tracking, and tax-ready reports. Export to Excel or share with investors in one click.
Vacancy Marketing
Auto-syndicate listings to property portals, generate AI-written descriptions, and schedule virtual tours directly from the platform.
Tenant Portal
Self-service portal for tenants to pay rent, raise issues, view statements, and access documents — reducing your support load by 70%.
Investor Reports
White-label investor portals with ROI dashboards, distribution tracking, and automated quarterly reports — impress every stakeholder.
