How Lipa Mdogo Works

A flexible settlement plan letting tenants pay rent and bills in smaller portions across the month, rather than a single lump sum — the same total, differently paced.
Two, four, or six instalments per month. The ledger calculates each portion and its due date automatically once you choose.
None. Lipa Mdogo is free for Mwarokin Estates tenants — you pay exactly the same total, simply spread across the month.
We send reminders ahead of each due date. A 3-day grace period follows any missed instalment before a small late fee applies.
