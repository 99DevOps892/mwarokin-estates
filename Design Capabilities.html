The studio toolkit

Everything a drafting table does — without the tracing paper.

Each tool is built around a real step in getting a plot approved and built, from first massing to the final material spec sheet.

01

Immersive massing

Rotate, zoom and walk a plot in real 3D with photoreal shadow falloff, so a client sees the house before the first block is laid.

02

Precision drafting

Every wall, opening and roof pitch is dimensioned to spec, so what you approve on screen matches what the contractor builds on site.

03

Light studies

Preview a plot at 7am and 5pm equatorial sun to check glare, shading and where the veranda actually stays cool.

04

Material library

Swap between murram brick, coral stone, cedar and glass presets calibrated against real Kenyan suppliers, not generic renders.

05

Live iteration

Change a material or footprint and the whole massing updates in the same frame — no re-export, no waiting on a render queue.

06

Shared review

Architect, contractor and homeowner mark up the same model live — comments stay pinned to the wall or window they're about.
