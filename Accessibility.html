Assistive technology

Built for every ability

Advanced screen reader

Natural voice synthesis with customisable speed, pitch, and language. Navigate every element by keyboard with rich ARIA labels throughout.

Voice navigation

Say "find properties", "apply now", or "go to payments" and navigate the entire platform hands-free with context-aware commands.

Vision customisation

High contrast, colour-blind safe, low vision, and dyslexia-friendly modes. Adjust text size, spacing, and tracking at any time.

Motor accessibility

Switch access, eye tracking integration, sip-and-puff compatibility, and customisable gesture controls for users with limited motor ability.

Hearing support

Visual alerts, real-time captions, sign language overlay options, and vibration feedback for all audio content and notifications.

Cognitive support

Simplified layouts, reading guides, distraction-free mode, plain-language summaries, and step-by-step guided application flows.
